Delete your Taco Please account and data

Option 1 – in the game

Open Taco Please, go to Settings, open your account and choose "Delete account". This deletes your player account and your cloud save straight away.

Option 2 – by email

Send an email to UBTOPstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Taco Please account deletion" from the email address you want us to reply to. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your player account (anonymous player ID) and sign-in link.
  • Your cloud save (game progress, purchase records kept in the cloud).

What is kept

  • Anonymous analytics and crash data that cannot be linked to you, for up to 14 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports).
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's policies.

Game progress saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.

Eliminar tu cuenta de Taco Please

Desde el juego: abre Ajustes, entra a tu cuenta y elige "Eliminar cuenta". Tu cuenta y tu guardado en la nube se eliminan al instante.

O escríbenos a UBTOPstudio@gmail.com con el asunto "Taco Please account deletion" y eliminaremos tu cuenta en un plazo de 30 días.

Se elimina: tu cuenta de jugador y tu guardado en la nube. Se conserva: datos anónimos de análisis y reportes de errores por hasta 14 meses y 90 días, respectivamente.
