Privacy Policy – Taco Please

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Español · If the English and Spanish versions differ, the English version prevails.

Thank you for playing Taco Please, a mobile game by UBTOP Studio ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect and Why

Taco Please does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or physical address. The game and its partners use limited technical information:

InformationPurposeLegal basis
Advertising ID, device type and model, operating system versionShowing ads; personalized ads only if you acceptYour consent
Gameplay statistics and analytics eventsUnderstanding how the game is played and improving itYour consent
Crash reports and performance dataFixing bugs and crashesYour consent
Anonymous player ID and game progress (only if you sign in)Saving and restoring your progress in the cloudProviding the service you asked for

This information does not identify you by name. We do not sell, trade or rent personal information to third parties.

2. Partners

  • Google Play Services – app delivery and purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – advertisements. How Google uses information from ads
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and crash reporting) – game statistics and crash reports. Firebase privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads – advertisements. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity Gaming Services – sign-in and cloud save. Unity Privacy Policy

3. Consent and Your Choices

When you first open the game, we ask whether you agree to personalized ads and analytics. Until you choose "Accept", personalized ads, ad measurement and analytics are off; the game still works and may show non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads on most Android phones).

4. International Transfers

Our partners process data on servers outside your country, mainly in the United States and the European Union. They protect it under their data-processing terms and standard contractual clauses. Where the law of your country requires it, your consent in the game covers this transfer.

5. Data Retention

  • Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.
  • Cloud save and player account: kept until you delete them in Settings, or ask us to.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to give you a copy of it, to stop processing it for advertising or profiling, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. In Mexico these are your ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation and opposition) under the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country, for example in Mexico the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, in Argentina the AAIP, in Colombia the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio, in Chile the data protection agency, or in Peru the ANPD. California residents can ask what personal information we hold and ask us to delete it; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without your consent.

7. Permissions

Taco Please only requests permissions necessary for normal game operation. The game does not access contacts, photos or media files, microphone, camera, or location data, unless explicitly required by platform services.

8. Children

Taco Please is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before accepting personalized ads and analytics; without that agreement, choose "Only necessary". A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's data by email.

9. Security

We use the security measures of our partners' platforms and do not intentionally collect sensitive device data. The game does not engage in malicious behavior, deceptive practices, or unauthorized device or network access.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page with a new date and tell you in the game about important changes.
